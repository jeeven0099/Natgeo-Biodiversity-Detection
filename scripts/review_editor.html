no file open: open one, drop one here, or record
Opening a file reads it in this browser tab; save WAV downloads the edited copy, and nothing is written to the project. detect calls is the one exception: it sends the file (the original bytes if unedited, else the edit as a 32-bit float WAV) to data/uploads, where the zero-shot model runs on it and the box review lists it. Answers given there are logged as usual, and the learners count them like any upload's.
How it works
Drag on the spectrogram to select a stretch of time (all frequencies); drag a yellow edge to move it; click to put the cursor there. Ctrl+X/Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V cut, copy, paste (paste replaces the selection, else goes in at the cursor) · Del deletes the selection · Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Ctrl+A select all · Esc clear the selection · Space play/stop · R record/stop.
Wheel zooms in time about the mouse · Shift+wheel pans · Shift+drag or middle-button drag pans · the box at the top right reads the time, frequency and level under the mouse.
A recording goes in like a paste: over the selection, else at the cursor; with no file open it becomes a new file. The microphone is read raw (no echo cancelling, noise suppression or automatic gain). A clip at another sample rate is resampled to the file's rate; mono and stereo are matched to the file's channels. WAV files are read at their own sample rate; other formats are decoded by the browser at its rate. The clipboard lives in this tab only. Undo keeps up to 50 M samples of changes.
